How It Works

AI-powered horror, every single night.

SweetyStoryLab runs on a fully automated pipeline that generates and publishes unique horror content daily — no human intervention required after setup.

1

Story Generation

Groq AI invents a unique horror story set anywhere in the world — drawing from folklore, urban legends, and paranormal traditions.

2

Visual Creation

Pollinations AI generates three cinematic horror images — one per scene — with eerie atmospheres and dramatic lighting.

3

Voice Narration

Edge TTS narrates the story with a voice that matches the narrator's gender — male or female — for full immersion.

4

Video Assembly

FFmpeg assembles the 3-scene slideshow with crossfade transitions, burned subtitles, and suspense background music.

5

Auto Publishing

The final Reel is automatically uploaded and published to Facebook and TikTok every night at 7PM PHT via their respective APIs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How often do you post?

Once daily at 7PM Philippine Time (PHT) on both Facebook and TikTok.

Are the stories real?

All stories are original AI-generated fiction for entertainment purposes only. Any resemblance to real events is coincidental.

Where do the stories come from?

Stories are generated by Groq AI, drawing from horror traditions worldwide — Japanese urban legends, Filipino folklore, Latin American mythology, and more.

Is the content appropriate for all ages?

Our content is intended for audiences 18 and older. Stories focus on psychological fear with no graphic violence or gore.

How can I connect my TikTok account?

Click the Connect to TikTok button in the platforms section above to authorize SweetyStoryLab to post on your behalf.
