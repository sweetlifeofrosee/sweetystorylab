Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Overview

SweetyStoryLab ("we", "our", "us") operates automated horror content pages on Facebook and TikTok. This policy explains how we handle data in connection with our content automation tools and this website.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data from viewers. Our automated system processes:

  • AI-generated story content (no personal data)
  • Publicly available platform engagement metrics
  • Internal post logs (title, timestamp, status)

3. Third-Party Services

Our automation uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Groq API — AI story generation
  • ElevenLabs — AI voice narration
  • Pollinations.ai — AI image generation
  • Facebook Graph API — content publishing
  • TikTok Content Posting API — content publishing

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts of any kind.

5. Children's Privacy

Our content is intended for audiences 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has interacted with our content inappropriately, please contact us.

6. Content

All stories published by SweetyStoryLab are original AI-generated fiction. Any resemblance to real events, locations, or persons is entirely coincidental.

7. Data Retention

We retain internal post logs (story titles, timestamps, publish status) indefinitely for operational purposes. No personal viewer data is stored.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data from viewers, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have concerns, please reach out via our Facebook Page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact

For privacy concerns or questions, reach us through our Facebook Page.
